How review signals are calculated

Rising volume: at least 20 complaints in the recent 365 days, at least 10 expected from the preceding 84-day baseline, volume at least 1.5× expected, and at least 10 excess complaints. Expected = baseline count ÷ 3.

Late responses: at least 30 recent complaints with known timeliness, at least 3 marked “No”, and late share at least 10%.

Rules are adjustable investigation filters, not validated risk scores, statistical significance tests or regulatory standards. Newest 60 receipt-date days are excluded as a publication-lag assumption. Seasonality, publicity and customer counts are not controlled. “Timely” describes the published response flag; it does not measure satisfaction or resolution time. Company and issue labels are preserved, with whitespace trimmed; no fuzzy entity matching is applied.

Limits

Credit card product only, receipt dates from 2023. Legacy “Credit card or prepaid card” records are outside this scope. Public complaints are not a representative customer sample. Publication and response changes can revise historical counts. The pipeline preserves correction history and raw snapshots. Recent publication lag cannot be eliminated merely by selecting a 60-day exclusion. No complaints are treated as proven misconduct.
